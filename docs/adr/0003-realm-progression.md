# ADR 0003 — Irreversible realm choice

Status: explicitly accepted by the user, 2026-09-19.

After Act I the party chooses Heaven or Hell once. Heaven cannot switch to Hell, and Hell cannot switch to Heaven. Act II follows that route and Act III culminates in its final realm (Empyrean/Heaven or Abyss/Hell). There is no mixed-route Purgatory in the accepted product scope. This supersedes section 6 and the conflicting mixed-route content in the supplied master specification.

Only Act I has Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. Later acts use realm-specific radial themes, superseding Appendix C's seasonal layers per act. The boss altar belongs to the outer progression zone; Winter is its Act I instance.

No backpack or stash: only equipment and persistent-within-act world items, as specified. That system is scheduled after the combat milestone.
